Valley Estates support

Ferguson Valley farming, from Red Gum Interactive.

Answers for the Android app, the iOS app, and the web game. Back to the game · Privacy policy

How do I play?

Tap a farmhand in a straw hat, then tap a tree, rock, berry bush, or building to put them to work. Drag to pan and pinch to zoom. The 🎯 card at the top left lists the current goal.

Open 🔨 to build. Cottages raise the population. Wheat fields and a barn keep food coming in. A vineyard grows grapes, and a winery turns four grapes into one bottle. A paddock holds Speckle Park cattle or Babydoll sheep.

Advance through the Homestead, Settler, Estate, and Valley Empire ages from the homestead. Seasons bring drought, frost, foxes, and bushfire. You win by reaching the Valley Empire Age and bottling 60 wines.

Sound and the silent switch

The first tap unlocks sound. Open the ☰ menu for Ambience, Effects, and Voice sliders, and for Mute. Those settings are stored on the device under valleyEstatesAudio, separate from the estate save. Mute is off unless you turn it on.

On iPhone and iPad the game uses the playback audio session, so the hardware silent switch does not mute Valley Estates. Use Mute in the menu, or turn the media volume down, if you want quiet. Sound pauses while the app is in the background.

Where is my save?

The estate is stored only on that device, in local storage under the key valleyEstatesV4. The save holds the map, buildings, resources, and camera. The game writes it about every 10 seconds. It is not sent to Red Gum Interactive or to anyone else, and it is not tied to an account.

A phone backup, such as iCloud or Android backup, may include that local storage. That backup is controlled by you and by the platform.

How do I reset?

Open the ☰ menu and choose New Game. That erases the estate save on this device and starts a new homestead. Sound settings stay as they are.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the browser’s site data for this page, removes the save and the sound settings. A deleted estate cannot be restored, because no copy is kept.
