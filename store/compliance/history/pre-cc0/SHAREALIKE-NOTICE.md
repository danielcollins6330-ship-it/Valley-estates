## Moving-image synchronization also needs review

These provisions are not limited to musical compositions. The 3.0 definition of Adaptation refers to a musical work, performance or phonogram synchronized with a moving image. The 4.0 definition says Adapted Material is always produced where the licensed musical work, performance or sound recording is synchronized in timed relation with a moving image. The full texts below govern the exact language.

Do not dismiss these clauses merely because a clip is a dog bark or bird call rather than music. Their application to the app's audiovisual presentation and a reviewer-accessible demonstration recording requires assessment. In particular, do not assert that ShareAlike can only ever apply to the two standalone files, or assume that making a video creates no additional obligations. Technical format changes alone also should not be treated as a definitive copyright-law test for adaptation.
