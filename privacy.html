Valley Estates privacy policy

App id com.littleridgeestate.valleyestates. Effective 20 October 2026.

Valley Estates is a farming strategy game set in Ferguson Valley, Western Australia. This policy covers the Android app, the iOS app, and the web version of the game.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracker. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.

The game does not send gameplay, device identifiers, or diagnostics to us or to anyone else. It is playable offline. The packaged app loads its files from the device. It does not download gameplay content.

Saves stay on your device

Your estate is stored only in local storage on the device or browser, under the key valleyEstatesV4. That save includes the map, buildings, resources, and camera position. It never leaves the device as part of the game.

Choosing New Game deletes that local save. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the browser’s site data, deletes it as well. We cannot restore a lost save because we never receive a copy.

Your phone or computer’s own backup (for example iCloud device backup or Android backup) may include the app’s local storage. That backup is controlled by you and by the platform, not by us.

What the game does not do

  • No purchases, subscriptions, or virtual currency bought with real money.
  • No ads and no ad identifiers.
  • No social features, chat, or user-generated content shared with other players.
  • No precise or coarse location. “Ferguson Valley” is the setting of the game, not a reading from your device.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Winemaking appears as a farm product. The game does not depict drinking or intoxication.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated page will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
